**Role Summary**
We are seeking an experienced
**Business Consultant – AI/ML Engineer**
to design, develop, and deploy scalable Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning solutions. The ideal candidate should have strong hands-on experience with Python, machine learning frameworks, data processing, and cloud-based AI/ML services.
  
  
The role will collaborate with data scientists, data engineers, application developers, and business stakeholders to build production-ready ML solutions and integrate AI capabilities into enterprise applications.
  
  
**Day To Day Job Duties**

* Design, develop, train, and deploy Machine Learning models for enterprise use cases.
* Develop data preprocessing, feature engineering, model training, and evaluation pipelines.
* Build and integrate AI/ML services with enterprise applications using APIs and Microservices.
* Evaluate ML algorithms and optimize models for accuracy, performance, and scalability.
* Develop automated ML pipelines for model training, testing, deployment, and monitoring.
* Collaborate with Data Scientists, Data Engineers, and business teams to translate requirements into AI/ML solutions.
* Implement MLOps practices for model versioning, deployment, monitoring, and lifecycle management.
* Work with structured and unstructured datasets from multiple sources.
* Monitor production models and address model performance, drift, and data-quality issues.
* Participate in code reviews, technical design discussions, and Agile delivery activities.

**Basic Qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience in Python development, Data Science, or Machine Learning engineering.
* 3+ years of experience developing ML solutions using Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, or similar frameworks.
* 3+ years of experience with data processing, feature engineering, SQL, and model development.
* 2+ years of experience deploying AI/ML solutions on AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform.

**Nice to Have**

* Experience with Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Agentic AI.
* Knowledge of RAG, embeddings, vector databases, and semantic search.
* Experience with LLM frameworks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, or similar technologies.
* Experience with Azure OpenAI, Amazon Bedrock, Vertex AI, or similar AI platforms.
* Knowledge of MLOps tools and model lifecycle management.
* Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines.
* Familiarity with Spark, Databricks, Snowflake, or other enterprise data platforms.
* Understanding of responsible AI, model governance, security, and data privacy.
* Experience developing AI solutions within Banking, Financial Services, Healthcare, Retail, or other enterprise domains.
* Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills.
